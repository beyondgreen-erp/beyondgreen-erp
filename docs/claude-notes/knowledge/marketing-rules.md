# Marketing Rules

## Claims (strict)
- Never "biodegradable" or degradation language.
- Describe products as an alternative to traditional petroleum-based plastics.
- Public language on own-brand products: brand name plus certifications only. Check certification backing before any compostable claim.
- Leave BPI/compostable claims out of social marketing; hint at the environmental angle through the beyondGREEN name.
- Straws are not TUV AUSTRIA certified. Cutlery is not CPLA. Cups are made at the India sister facility.
- USDA BioPreferred: present at company level.
- Amazon: functional descriptions and "Made in USA" only.

## Positioning
- Lead with Made in USA (and Made in California), low MOQs, U.S. manufacturing near SNA, distributor coverage, supplier credentials.
- Do not call beyondGREEN a "small manufacturer".
- Price-beat wording: "competitor quote match or beat", no competitor names.

## Channels and style
- Daily presence across all channels, tied to the cold outreach lists.
- LinkedIn posts from Rudy's personal profile, third person about beyondGREEN.
- Brand should look extremely professional; imagery generated with Artlist; no text on photos for the website.
- Website and new-site visual rules: see websites-and-ecommerce.md.
