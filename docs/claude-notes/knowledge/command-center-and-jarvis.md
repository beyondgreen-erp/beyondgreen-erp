# Command Center (Spica) and the old Jarvis

## History
- Jarvis (repo beyondgreen-erp/Jarvis) was the earlier ERP assistant; it did not work well and was paused/scrapped in Oct 2026 (a copy is kept).
- Replaced by a new standalone Command Center app; prototype (3D "bridge" look) approved, then built for real. Single login for Rudy.

## Purpose
- Rudy's centralized work area across beyondGREEN, WildKind, Epsilon Pacific and personal tasks.
- Goal: "100% automation with his approval". A full operating Claude inside it handling email, ERP and Shopify changes, files, research, social and outreach.
- Must ask first before anything that sends, posts, pays, deletes or changes data.
- Should post into the ERP and show every ERP change, comment and update.
- Model choice (Oct 31, 2026): smart mix, Opus for real tasks, Haiku for quick chat. Show usage and cost.

## Spica (voice assistant)
- Wakes by name, open conversation he can interrupt; must not hear herself without headphones. ElevenLabs voice.
- Executive-assistant persona: warm peer, short voice-first replies, proactive next steps, plans then waits for "go". Added lenses: Growth Partner (scaling operator) and Performance Marketing.
- Full web access; can create ERP records (e.g., sample requests).

## Inbox and approvals
- Connect his inboxes directly (not forwarding). Delete mail, flag "waiting on", sign PDFs from emails and attach signed copies to drafts.
- Never auto-create reminders/tasks from emails. Ask whether he wants a reminder, task or sample submission, only for real conversations with a pending deliverable; nothing for cold outbound unless someone replies, nothing for old emails.
- Consolidate threads; do not repeat the same approval or log the same contact twice.
- Customer/lead emails are logged as conversations under the ERP customer after approval.
- Approvals shown as an accordion. Reminders show the linked email.

## Projects and marketing
- Earlier Workroom/Team pages were rebuilt into one Projects board with what-was-done results, server-side automation and approvals that execute. Tasks are not done until Rudy says so.
- Marketing: beyondGREEN visible daily on all channels, tied to cold outreach; posts approved once per day. Social posting must be free and direct (no paid schedulers). Accounts: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn (from his personal profile), X; Pinterest to be created; TikTok/YouTube API review pending.
- Marketing imagery via Artlist using his best-performing visuals as art direction. No paid marketing for the straw launch.
- Leave BPI/compostable claims out of marketing; hint at the environmental angle through the name.

## UI and security
- Should look and work like the beyondGREEN ERP. Black background. Minimal text; explanations pop up on click. He finds clutter confusing and wants to see what changed.
- Security: 2-step sign-in (authenticator), encrypted stored keys, security headers. No auto sign-out.
