# Products and Engineering

## Resins (all BASF ecovio)
- FS1606: straws. IA1542: cutlery (60/20/20 blend of IA1542 / PLA / regrind). F23B1: natural film bags. F2330: clear film bags.
- PHA is no longer used (straws cracked); BASF PBAT+PLA blends replaced it.
- Sourcing injection-grade PLA from China (see sourcing.md).

## Cutlery
- Injection molded in Santa Ana. Certified compostable, NOT CPLA.
- Cutlery kits: kitting machine in Santa Ana; kit film wrapper is completely clear.
- Compostable spork: CAD package at REV D (152 mm OAL, ribbed design). Mold carries BPI embossing; company name goes on the box, not the spork. Mold testing in Santa Ana planned for Nov 2026; Jan 2027 is the safe availability estimate.
- Manufacturing cost model built for the cutlery line (cycle-time and machine-rate sensitivity). Do not put cost figures in shared docs.

## Straws
- Profile extruded (not molded); wrappers are paper. Straw wrap film is produced in-house.
- Sizes: standard, jumbo, giant, cocktail, and bendy (straw bending machine). Wrapped or unwrapped; black, white, green; custom colors and printed branded wrappers available.
- Do not imply custom colors are only for large runs.

## Film bags
- Monolayer compostable biopolymer extrusion: take-out, produce/grocery, trash liners, food waste, pet waste. Standard take-out bag color is green.
- Mylar/barrier work goes to the family's other facility.

## Paper products
- Kraft bags: custom printing in the U.S., 250-bag minimum, about 10-day lead time; 5 sizes in stock.
- Cups: aqueous coated, single and double wall; India now, Santa Ana lines coming Q1 2027.
- Clamshells: aqueous coated, certified home and industrial compostable.

## Lead times (customer-facing)
- U.S.-made items: 2 to 3 weeks. India custom products: 70 to 90 days.
