# Walmart Great Value Program

## Overview
- beyondGREEN supplies Walmart Great Value (GV) compostable cutlery. Supplier agreement is evergreen.
- 7 SKUs tracked: F24, A24, K48, A48, S48, S24, F48 (F = fork, S = spoon, K = knife, A = assorted; 24 or 48 count).
- A FY28 deletion of all 7 items was announced, then Walmart reversed course (late Sep 2026); program continues.
- Store expansions supported in Fall 2026 (48CT comp items, and a large 24ct spoon expansion through WK20).
- Oct 2026: FY27 Sourcing RFI submitted in Coupa for new categories (film bags, pet waste, straws, cups, bowls, napkins).

## Terminology and reporting (strict)
- "SRPs" = 6-pack display/shelf-ready packs. "Unit Packaging" = individual unit boxes. Never say "trays".
- All reporting is in units (packs), never SRPs.
- Weekly GV program update email to the Walmart buyer team with a workbook: Dashboard, Weekly Orders, Safety Stock, Forecast, Production, Order vs Ship, Packaging tabs. Safety stock rolls forward from the prior report's closing position; Rudy supplies the week's data.
- Packaging depletion priority: Unit Packaging first; SRP stock stays current with demand.
- Weekly In-Stock Recovery Request (through the holidays) for items under the 98% in-stock goal: fill recovery week, root cause, recovery plan, owner; return before Walmart's Monday deadline and flag blockers the same day.

## Shipping and Stride (TMS)
- Orders ship Collect, picked up by Walmart Transportation; POs confirmed in Stride.
- Nothing can be double stacked: each pallet is its own position. Max safe pallet height 60 in including deck.
- SRPs have no master cartons, so even the smallest PO ships on a pallet.
- Chargebacks have been cube-mismatch/routing claims. Stride entries must be calculated, not measured from built pallets (pallets are built after loads are assigned).
- Interim method (Oct 31, 2026, until Walmart Transportation answers): POs of 2+ pallets as PALLET with one position per pallet and DO NOT STACK labels; single-pallet and small POs as FLOOR at the cube of a full pallet position; gross weight includes pallets and BOL matches. Correct Walmart's pre-filled Stride values before confirming.
- Rudy has asked Walmart to return to staple-stock POs in original full-pallet quantities (e.g., 48CT spoon and fork = 120 SRPs per pallet).

## Compliance and quality
- Public language on beyondGREEN-labeled products: brand name plus certifications only; no degradation/biodegradability messaging.
- Made in California mark to be added to GV packaging only after current packaging is consumed.
- Walmart accepts compostable packaging bags if the BPI logo and certification number are printed.
- Walmart Quality Monitoring Program: a third-party lab periodically requests GV samples; the team handles these, and the test reports are useful for all customers.
- Walmart orders are entered in the ERP by the operations team.
