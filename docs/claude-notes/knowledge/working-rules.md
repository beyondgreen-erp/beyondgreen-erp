# Working Rules (Rudy's standing preferences)

Apply these to every task unless Rudy says otherwise in the current request.

## Writing
- USA English spelling everywhere ("color", never "colour"), including ERP text, videos and documents.
- No em dashes in drafted writing. Use commas, colons or periods.
- Emails: phrase asks politely ("Could you please send over...", not "Send over...").
- When Rudy pastes an email thread with no other instruction, draft his reply to it.

## Delivery
- Rudy cannot open .md files. Deliver documents and reports to him as PDF or Word.
- He is often on his phone: keep replies short and say what was done, not every step.
- Keep a .md knowledge record for every task (for Claude's own reference, not for Rudy). Add a dated note to `docs/claude-notes/` after each task and update the relevant file in this folder when something durable is learned.

## Walmart terminology and reporting
- Display trays (6-pack shelf-ready packs) are "SRPs". Never call them "trays".
- Individual unit boxes are "Unit Packaging".
- All Walmart reporting (emails, workbooks, charts) is in units (packs), never in SRPs.

## Die line format (standard since Oct 2026, based on the bendy straw wrapper die line)
- One landscape letter page.
- Green beyondGREEN header band, logo in all white with no white box behind it.
- Title "DIE LINE: <ITEM>" with revision and date.
- Die drawn at actual size with dimension arrows in inches and mm.
- Magenta cut line, red dashed bleed, green dashed safe area.
- Cards below the drawing: Specifications; Line Key + Artwork Requirements; Notes + Approval.
- Company address footer.
- File it in the ERP Document Repository under Drawings & Dielines.

## Company documents
- Build formal company documents (e.g., technical/compliance packages) on the July 2026 Word letterhead (white page, green "b" logo, company block top right, green rule), not the older green-band letterhead. It is filed in the ERP.
