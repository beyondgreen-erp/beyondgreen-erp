# beyondGREEN Company Overview

## Basics
- beyondGREEN biotech, Inc., 1202 E. Wakeham Ave., Santa Ana, CA 92705. Web: beyondgreenbiotech.com (points to byndgrn.com).
- Manufacturer of compostable foodservice and packaging products: cutlery, cutlery kits, straws (incl. bendy), film bags, cups, films.
- Co-founded by Rudy Patel (Chief Business Development Officer) with his father Veejay Patell (CEO).
- History: founded 2016 as bioDOGradable Bags; U.S. manufacturing began in Lake Forest in 2018; became beyondGREEN biotech, Inc. in 2020; now in Santa Ana. A former San Antonio, TX plant was consolidated into Santa Ana.
- About 30 FTEs, all in California. Lean admin team; everyone wears multiple hats.
- Santa Ana factory is under 15 minutes from John Wayne Airport (SNA).

## Related entities
- beyondGREEN Professional LLC (bG-Pro): partner channel with its own portal and brand.
- ForeverGreen Tech LLC: compostable straws affiliate; also the channel through which Sysco is supplied.
- India sister facility: makes paper products (cups, food boats, clamshells, liner paper, paper bags). Call it "our sister facility" in marketing.
- Flexible packaging (mylar, barrier laminates, printed pouches) is handled at the family's other facility in City of Industry, CA. Refer to it externally as "our other family operated facility".
- Epsilon Pacific LLC (Rudy's sourcing advisory firm) acts as importer/consignee on some India shipments for beyondGREEN.

## What is made where
- Santa Ana (USA): cutlery, cutlery kits, straws (profile extruded), monolayer compostable film bags (take-out, produce, trash), napkins, custom printed kraft bags. Can also run polystyrene cutlery.
- India: cups and other paper products. Do not describe cups as U.S.-made.
- Two paper cup conversion lines are being installed in Santa Ana (aqueous coated, single and double wall, 8 to 24 oz); target running by Q1 2027. Until then, only select double-wall cup sizes ship from the U.S.
- Made-in-USA line (the only items offered to U.S. government/military buyers): cutlery, cutlery kits, straws, film products such as trash bags.
- 2026 equipment additions: a cutlery kitting machine and a straw bending machine (one of very few in the U.S.).

## Certifications and credentials
- BPI; TUV AUSTRIA OK compost HOME and INDUSTRIAL (film and cutlery); USDA BioPreferred (company level; cutlery family registered Oct 2026, label status in process; pursuing the double label for product plus packaging).
- Cutlery home compost: TUV Rheinland / DIN CERTCO approved (Oct 2026); BPI Home in process; TUV AUSTRIA home pending.
- Straws: commercial/industrial composting only; NOT TUV AUSTRIA certified. Bendy straws in BPI process; BPI-listed straws are straight straws.
- Cups: industrial certified, home certification in process; BPI tied to producing facility, so U.S. cup certification waits for U.S. production.
- Made in California certification received Oct 2026.
- GMP and HACCP certified; NMSDC certified minority-owned supplier; Qualified Opportunity Zone Business; Green America certified business.
- All materials at both facilities are FDA compliant and EU food-contact approved.
- Does NOT hold a California DGS Small Business/DVBE certification (pursuing it). Not in Avendra currently.

## Language and claims rules
- Never use "biodegradable" or similar degradation terms.
- Describe products as an alternative to traditional petroleum-based plastics; be conservative with compostability claims and check certification backing before publishing.
- Do not print compostable claims/marks on own stock items for now (California guidance); private label runs can carry them if the customer wants.
- Cutlery is certified compostable but NOT CPLA.
- Do not call beyondGREEN a "small manufacturer".
- Stock cutlery and straws carry the green/brown markings required by Washington and Colorado labeling laws.
- Do not contact Veritiv. Decline scented bag requests.
