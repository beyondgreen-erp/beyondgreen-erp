# Sales Outreach: Approach and Rules

## General rules for all outreach emails
- USA English, no em dashes, polite asks.
- Never "biodegradable"; never call cutlery CPLA; cups are made in India (sister facility).
- Pitch made in USA, low MOQs (name bag types: kraft take-out, produce/grocery, food waste, trash liners), supplier credentials (GMP/HACCP, NMSDC minority-owned, QOZB, Green America, Made in California, USDA BioPreferred).
- Competitive edge as the catalog presents it: direct or through distributor, "competitor quote match or beat" (never "we will beat it", never name competitors; like-for-like written quote from another U.S.-based supplier within 30 days), rebates/GPO, free pilot (first case free), free samples, branded wrappers, stock and release, Switch Savings Report, distributor/reseller program.
- Attach all 8 flyers plus the beyondGREEN Product Guide, and include the drinking straws link: https://byndgrn.com/pages/drinking-straws
- Do not cite former customers (SONIC, AMC, Live Nation) as current. Marriott only for cups.
- Always skip: Veritiv, Wawa, Puttshack, big centrally-bought chains, recruiters, non-food businesses.
- Sample requests go to the person who ships sample boxes; log everything in the ERP and Command Center.

## Process
- Prospect via Apollo.io (Apollo-only email lookups, verified emails only). Get Rudy's OK on credit spend before enrichment. Do not add new contacts to Apollo lists (extra credit charges).
- Send as Outlook drafts, not Apollo sequences: drafts first, Rudy approves, then send.
- Dedupe every batch against ERP (customer_outreach, customers), earlier batches and domains already emailed.
- Contact every buyer at a target organization, not just one or two.
- After sending: check bounces 1 to 2 days later, draft replies same day, one follow-up 7 to 10 days later (max 2 touches), keep a scorecard (sent, bounced, replied, samples, quotes, orders).
- Deliverability: after heavy sending caused spam placement (Oct 2026), stay on the own domain at low volume and ramp slowly. No lookalike domains.
